GOD OF WAR III — COMMUNITY ARM64 EDITION 0.4.1

ENGLISH
1. Move God of War III.app to Applications and open it.
2. Select your extracted game dump containing eboot.bin, sce_module and sce_sys.
   PKG/ISO extraction and decryption are not performed by this app.
3. Choose resolution, MetalFX and frame cap, then click Play. Wait for the first
   preparation to finish. It compiles native ARM64 code on your Mac and starts
   the game automatically. No Terminal, Xcode, Homebrew, Python installation,
   Rosetta or network connection is needed for preparation.
4. The folder, language and settings are remembered. The prepared result is
   reused. A new render resolution/frame-rate profile may need another short
   preparation; unchanged compiled objects are reused.
Cancel stops preparation safely. Click Play to retry; completed work is retained.

Requirements: Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later. Tested on M3 Max.
Tested executable: God of War III Remastered CUSA01623 v1.02. Compatibility is
checked using executable and module SHA-256 hashes, not just the SFO version.
Other regions, updates and modified executables are rejected with an explanation.
The tested dump's SFO reports APP_VER 01.00, despite its tested v1.02 executable.
Allow at least 3 GB of free Mac disk space for initial preparation. Game data stays
in the selected folder; no game dump, saves or generated game code is included.

This build is ad-hoc signed, not signed with Developer ID or notarized by Apple.
macOS may require approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
Only approve a download whose origin you trust. Do not disable Gatekeeper.
https://support.apple.com/en-us/102445

PORTUGUÊS BRASILEIRO
1. Mova God of War III.app para Aplicativos e abra.
2. Escolha seu dump extraído com eboot.bin, sce_module e sce_sys.
   O aplicativo não extrai nem descriptografa PKG/ISO.
3. Escolha as opções e clique em Jogar. Aguarde a preparação inicial: o código
   ARM64 é compilado neste Mac e o jogo inicia automaticamente ao terminar.

4. A pasta, o idioma e as configurações ficam lembrados. O resultado é reutilizado.
   Trocar a resolução interna ou o limite pode preparar outro perfil, reutilizando
   os objetos que não mudaram. Cancelar interrompe com segurança; Jogar retoma.

Requisitos: Apple Silicon, macOS 26 ou superior e pelo menos 3 GB livres para a
preparação. Testado no M3 Max, com CUSA01623 v1.02. O executável e os módulos são
conferidos por SHA-256; o número do SFO sozinho não garante compatibilidade.
Outras regiões, updates e executáveis modificados são recusados com uma explicação.
O pacote não inclui dump, saves nem código gerado do jogo. Os dados ficam externos.
Esta versão tem assinatura local, sem Developer ID/notarização. O macOS pode pedir
aprovação em Ajustes do Sistema > Privacidade e Segurança > Abrir Mesmo Assim.

   La aplicación no extrae ni descifra PKG/ISO.

Requisitos: Apple Silicon, macOS 26 o posterior, al menos 3 GB libres para preparar.
Probado en M3 Max con CUSA01623 v1.02. Se verifican hashes SHA-256 del ejecutable y
los módulos; el número del SFO no basta. Otras regiones/updates/mods son rechazados.

KEYBOARD / TECLADO
Space = Cross/X (confirm and jump); Enter = Options. Use Space to confirm the
first brightness screen. A controller uses its normal south face button.
Espaço confirma o X na tela de brilho inicial. Enter corresponde a Options.
Espacio confirma la X en la pantalla de brillo inicial. Enter es Options.

SETTINGS / CONFIGURAÇÕES / AJUSTES
720p, 1080p, 1440p, 4K; native or spatial MetalFX Quality/Balanced/Performance.
60 FPS is recommended. Unlimited uses the upstream 240 FPS timing profile while
retaining V-Sync; it is not a campaign-wide simulation-speed guarantee.
Retina, V-Sync and fullscreen/Game Mode metadata remain enabled. The OS controls
actual Game Mode and remembers a manual opt-out. Frame generation is disabled.
With the 60 FPS cap the app temporarily selects 60 Hz, then restores the previous
display mode on exit. Unlimited leaves the refresh mode unchanged.
O cap de 60 FPS usa 60 Hz temporariamente; Ilimitado preserva a escolha da tela.
El límite de 60 FPS usa 60 Hz temporalmente; Ilimitado conserva el modo de pantalla.
The game HUD always shows actual render dimensions and MetalFX mode/preset,
including when the optional Apple Metal performance HUD is off.

DATA / DADOS / DATOS
~/Library/Application Support/God of War III ARM64
Preferences, locally prepared code, saves, caches and logs live here, outside the
app. An update preserves them. Logs are available from the launcher's Open logs
button; preparation-stage logs are under Prepared/<recipe>/<dump>/Base or Profiles.
Exit through the game menu to save progress before using Stop game.
